# Project roadmap

This roadmap records intent, not a claim that a capability has shipped. Source,
migrations, and validation remain the evidence for implemented behavior.

## Direction

Town Hown is evolving from a controlled recruitment and assessment foundation
into a traceable people-operations platform: a confirmed hiring decision can
lead into onboarding; confirmed meeting outcomes can become owned work; and
management reporting can expose actionable, scope-safe signals.

## Priority themes

- Harden the recruitment and assessment flows, especially authorization,
  review gates, and operational reliability.
- Complete workflow links between offers, onboarding, meetings, tasks, and
  management reporting.
- Expand integrations and computer-vision-assisted monitoring only with an
  explicit provider contract, privacy controls, and human confirmation.
