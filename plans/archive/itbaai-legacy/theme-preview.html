Summary

Current Issues

  • Background #030305 is pure black causing eye strain
  • Cards at white/2% invisible against background
  • Text at white/40 fails WCAG AA contrast (2.5:1)
  • Hardcoded hex values prevent theming
  • Sci-fi jargon confuses HR users
  • Stat cards look clickable but have no interaction
  • No visual hierarchy between card states

Proposed Fixes

  • Blue-slate bg (#0C1220) with warm undertone
  • Surface (#151D2E) clearly elevated from bg
  • All text passes WCAG AA (4.5:1+ minimum)
  • CSS custom properties for full theme control
  • Professional HR-friendly language throughout
  • Distinct stat metrics with SVG icons and glow
  • Gradient shimmer hover states on cards
